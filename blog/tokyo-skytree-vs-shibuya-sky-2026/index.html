Tokyo Skytree vs SHIBUYA SKY

SKY
Two decks, pick oneChecked 2026-10-30

Facts checked 2026-10-30 · 4 min read · Route Lab

AI-assisted content: drafted with AI tools, then fact-checked and edited by Route Lab.

Most first-time visitors to Tokyo want one big view from above, and most end up choosing between two: Tokyo Skytree, the 634 m tower in Oshiage, and SHIBUYA SKY, the open-air rooftop on top of Shibuya Scramble Square. Both are worth it, but most people only need one.

The short version

  • Skytree has priced each day separately since April 1, 2026: Tembo Deck from ¥1,800, Deck + Galleria from ¥3,000 for adults.
  • Buying Skytree tickets at the counter adds ¥500 each, so book online.
  • SHIBUYA SKY web tickets cost ¥2,700 before 15:00 and ¥3,400 from 15:00, in 20-minute slots sold two weeks ahead.
  • Pick SHIBUYA SKY for an open-air sunset, Skytree for height, bad weather or an Asakusa evening.
  • Doing both costs ¥5,000 or more per adult before the Galleria, so most visitors only need one.

01Tokyo Skytree: per-day pricing from April 2026

Prices changed recently. Skytree dropped its fixed weekday and weekend prices on April 1, 2026, so a lot of older guides now quote the wrong numbers. Here's what each costs, how booking works, and how to choose. Everything was checked on the official sites on October 30, 2026.

Skytree announced that from April 1, 2026 it would drop its old ticket types (weekday/holiday and advance/same-day) and set a price for each day instead. That means there's no single "Skytree price". You see the real price for your date when you book on the official web booking site.

The official ticket page lists the starting prices:

  • Tembo Deck (350 m): adults (15+) from ¥1,800, children 6–14 from ¥900
  • Tembo Deck + Tembo Galleria (350 m and 450 m): adults from ¥3,000, children from ¥1,500
  • Tembo Galleria add-on: adults ¥1,400, children ¥700. You buy it on the day from machines inside the Tembo Deck, and only if you already have a Tembo Deck ticket. It can be suspended when the upper deck is crowded.

Two details catch people out:

  1. Buying at the 4th-floor counter or ticket machines adds ¥500 per ticket (the add-on above is exempt). Buy online.
  2. "From" really means from. Busy days cost more, so check the price for your date before you go.

Young children are free (age 5 and under, and preschoolers up to 6).

Skytree sits above Tokyo Solamachi, which also has Sumida Aquarium (¥2,700). That makes Skytree an easy rainy-day block, with the tower, the aquarium and lunch all under one roof.

02SHIBUYA SKY: timed tickets, open-air roof

SHIBUYA SKY sells date-and-time tickets in 20-minute entry slots. Its April 2025 price revision set these adult prices (age 12+):

Children aged 6–11 pay ¥1,200 for any slot, sold at the counter only, and children 5 and under are free. We confirmed the ¥3,400 web price for afternoon and evening slots in October 2026. Prices can change, so check before you go.

How booking works, from the same notice:

  • Web tickets go on sale two weeks before your date, at 00:00 Japan time.
  • Web tickets can be cancelled free until the day before. Counter tickets can't be cancelled.
  • The rooftop closes in rain, lightning, strong wind or extreme heat. If it's closed during your slot, you can cancel free that day before you enter. The indoor 46th-floor gallery stays open.
  • Large bags, including suitcases, aren't allowed, and there are limits on what you can take onto the roof.

The 15:00-and-later slots cost more because they cover sunset and the night view, and sunset slots sell out first. Our Tokyo plan books a 16:00 slot at least a week ahead.

03Which one should you pick?

Pick SHIBUYA SKY if:

  • You want an open-air view. The roof has no glass between you and the city.
  • You're already spending the afternoon in Shibuya or Harajuku.
  • You can book about two weeks ahead and want a sunset slot.

Pick Skytree if:

  • You want the highest view: 350 m, or 450 m with the Galleria.
  • The forecast is bad. It's fully indoors, and the aquarium is in the same building.
  • You're pairing it with Asakusa. Senso-ji and Skytree are close, and our plan does them together on the first evening.
  • You're travelling on a quiet weekday, when the per-day price is likely nearer the floor.

Do both? Only if views are your thing. Together they cost ¥5,000 or more per adult before the Galleria, and the views are of the same city from different sides.

04Cost at a glance (adults, official prices)

  • Skytree Tembo Deck: from ¥1,800 (per-day price; +¥500 at the counter)
  • Skytree Deck + Galleria: from ¥3,000 (per-day price)
  • SHIBUYA SKY, 10:00–14:59: ¥2,700 web (timed slot)
  • SHIBUYA SKY, 15:00–21:20: ¥3,400 web (sunset and night)

05Booking checklist

  1. Decide on the day and the neighbourhood first. The deck should fit your route, not the other way round.
  2. Skytree: check your date's price on the official booking site and buy online to avoid the ¥500 counter fee.
  3. SHIBUYA SKY: set a reminder for 00:00 Japan time, two weeks before. Check the rooftop status on the day.
  4. Have a rain backup ready, especially for SHIBUYA SKY, whose main attraction is the open roof.
