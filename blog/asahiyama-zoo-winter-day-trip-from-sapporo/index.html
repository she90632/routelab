Asahiyama Zoo in winter

AKJ
A zoo day that fits short hoursChecked 2026-10-30

Facts checked 2026-10-30 · 4 min read · Route Lab

AI-assisted content: drafted with AI tools, then fact-checked and edited by Route Lab.

Asahiyama Zoo in Asahikawa is one of the best-known zoos in Japan. Polar bears, seals and penguins live in enclosures built so you can see them up close. In winter it's also one of the easiest day trips from Sapporo. The catch is the clock: the zoo opens late and closes early in winter, so the train you catch decides the whole day.

The short version

  • Winter hours are 10:30–15:30 (last entry 15:00), Nov 11 – Dec 29, 2026 and Jan 2 – Apr 7, 2027.
  • Adults pay ¥1,000; children of junior high age and under are free. Advance tickets only skip the window line.
  • The penguin walk usually runs late December to mid-March, once there's enough snow. 2026–27 times aren't out yet.
  • From Sapporo: ¥5,440 each way by limited express, then bus 41 or 47 from stop 6 at Asahikawa Station for ¥670.

01Winter 2026–27 dates and hours

This guide covers the official 2026–27 winter dates, what entry costs, the penguin walk, and a simple day plan from Sapporo. Everything was checked on official sites on October 30, 2026.

According to the zoo's official calendar, the winter season runs in two blocks:

  • Nov 11 – Dec 29, 2026: 10:30–15:30, last entry 15:00
  • Jan 2 – Apr 7, 2027: 10:30–15:30, last entry 15:00

The zoo is closed Dec 30 – Jan 1. It's also closed between seasons: the summer season ends on Nov 3, 2026, and the zoo reopens for winter on Nov 11. In 2027 the summer season starts on Apr 29.

There's one exception to the short hours. On Feb 5–7, 2027, the zoo has a special evening opening, 10:30–20:30 (last entry 20:00). Those dates fall inside the Sapporo Snow Festival (Feb 4–11, 2027), so if you're in Hokkaido for the festival, one of those days gives you a much more relaxed zoo visit.

The zoo says a full visit takes about three hours, and longer with breaks, because it's built on a hillside. In winter you only get five hours between opening and closing, so arrive close to opening time.

02Tickets

  • Adults: ¥1,000.
  • Children (junior high school age and under): free.

You can buy at the gate, from ticket machines, or in advance online or at Seven-Eleven and Lawson convenience stores. The zoo says advance tickets work exactly like gate tickets: there's no booking or time slot. Their only advantage is skipping the ticket-window line. Advance tickets can't be cancelled or refunded, and a group that buys several web tickets together has to enter together.

03The penguin walk

The penguin walk is the image most people know: a line of king penguins waddling along a path through the snow. It isn't a show; the zoo runs it to give the penguins exercise in winter, and it only starts once there's enough snow to make the course.

The official FAQ says it usually runs from late December to mid-March. Last season (2025–26) it ran from Dec 18, at 11:00 and 14:30, then only at 11:00 from March while the penguins moult. The course is a round trip of about 500 m.

The 2026–27 start date and times haven't been announced yet. The zoo posts them on its website when the walks begin, so check before you go. Two rules apply: no flash photography, and don't step onto the course or throw snow. The zoo says it may stop the walk if visitors ignore the rules.

If you're visiting in November or the first half of December, plan to see the animals without the penguin walk.

04Getting there from Sapporo

Train: Sapporo to Asahikawa. The JR limited express Lilac or Kamui costs ¥5,440 one way with a reserved seat (¥3,080 fare + ¥2,360 reserved limited-express charge). That's ¥10,880 for the round trip. Check JR Hokkaido's timetable for exact departure times on your date.

Bus: Asahikawa Station to the zoo. Asahikawa Denki Kido runs buses 41 and 47 from stop 6 at Asahikawa Station. The fare is ¥670 one way for adults and ¥340 for children (new fares from Sept 1, 2026). The ride takes about 40–45 minutes. On this line, IC cards only work if they're the local Asaca or DoCARD; national IC cards like Suica aren't accepted. Ticket machines at the bus information centre also sell zoo tickets, so you can buy both at once.

The timetable we read covers the summer season (Apr 29 – Nov 3, 2026). The winter timetable wasn't posted when we checked, so check before you go on the bus company's timetable page.

If you're flying into Asahikawa Airport, bus 78 runs directly to the zoo in about 35 minutes for ¥840. It only runs on weekends and holidays while the zoo is open.

05A day plan that fits

This is the shape our Hokkaido itinerary uses for its zoo day:

  1. 08:00 Lilac/Kamui from Sapporo.
  2. 09:45 Bus 41 from stop 6 at Asahikawa Station.
  3. 10:30 Zoo opens. The zoo needs about three hours, so start straight away.
  4. 14:30 Bus back to Asahikawa Station. Last entry is 15:00 and the gates close at 15:30, so don't plan on the last minutes.
  5. 15:30 Asahikawa ramen near the station before the train back.
  6. 17:00 Limited express back to Sapporo.

Transport and entry for this day come to about ¥13,220 per adult: ¥10,880 in trains, ¥1,340 in buses and ¥1,000 for the zoo. Food isn't included.

06What to wear

You'll spend most of the day outdoors on a slope that's often packed snow. Waterproof boots with good grip matter more than anything else. Add warm layers, gloves and a hat. Our Hokkaido winter packing list has the full list.
