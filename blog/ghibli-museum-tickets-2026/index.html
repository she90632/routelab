Ghibli Museum tickets 2026

GBL
Sales day is the 10thChecked 2026-10-30

The Ghibli Museum in Mitaka is one of the hardest tickets in Tokyo to get. It isn't expensive. The difficulty is that you can't buy at the door, every ticket has a set entry time, and each month's tickets go on sale on one day. From November 2026 the ticket system has also changed, and every ticket now carries a small extra fee.

The short version

  • Adults pay ¥1,000, plus a new ¥110 system fee per ticket for entries from November 2026.
  • Tickets are sold online only, through Lawson Ticket, on the 10th of each month at 10:00 JST for the next month.
  • Tickets are named, max 6 per account per month, with no changes or refunds.
  • Entry slots run hourly 10:00–16:00; enter within an hour. Closed Tuesdays.
  • Don't buy from resellers: the museum says resold tickets won't get you in.

In words: ¥1,000 for adults and university students, ¥700 for high school and junior high students, ¥400 for elementary school children and ¥100 for preschoolers aged 4 and over. Children under 4 are free and don't need a ticket.

New from November 2026: the museum has moved to a QR-code ticket system, and for entries from November 2026 (tickets on sale from October 10) every ticket costs an extra ¥110. An adult ticket now comes to ¥1,110, and a family of four pays the fee four times. The museum says there are no group, advance or other discounts.

02Where and when to buy

  • Only online through Lawson Ticket (l-tike.com). The museum doesn't sell tickets at its own window.
  • Sales day: tickets for the following month go on sale on the 10th of each month at 10:00 Japan time. For example, December tickets go on sale on November 10.
  • You need a free Lawson web membership and a verified mobile phone number to buy.
  • Visitors outside Japan can also buy through the JTB Group or Lawson Ticket's English site. The museum lists both.

Lawson Ticket's rules for this museum are strict, so read them before the sales day:

  • Tickets are named. The name on your Lawson account appears on every ticket.
  • Buy with the account of someone who is actually visiting. Someone who isn't going can't buy on your behalf.
  • Each account can buy once a month, up to 6 tickets.
  • No changes to the name or date, and no refunds after purchase.
  • Reselling is banned, and resold tickets won't get you in.

Collecting tickets: choose either an electronic ticket, which from November 2026 you display through a link sent by email with no app needed (you do need a smartphone), or a paper ticket printed at a Lawson store's Loppi machine.

03Opening hours and entry times

  • Open 10:00–18:00. The last entry slot is 16:00.
  • Entry slots: 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00 and 16:00.
  • Enter within one hour of your slot. Once you're in, you can stay as long as you like. Visitors aren't swapped out at the next slot.
  • Closed Tuesdays. Other closures and long breaks change every year, and the museum says hours and days can change without notice, so check before you go.

Our Tokyo plan books the 10:00 slot. It gives you the whole morning inside, then the afternoon free for the park and Kichijoji.

04Booking strategy

  1. Know your sales day. Count back to the 10th of the month before your trip and set an alarm for 10:00 Japan time, converted to your time zone.
  2. Create the Lawson account and verify your phone beforehand. Don't leave it until the sales day.
  3. Have a first and second choice of date and time slot.
  4. Avoid Tuesdays when you plan the rest of your week around it.
  5. Budget ¥1,110 per adult, not ¥1,000.
  6. Arrive on time. You have one hour from your slot, and missing it can mean not getting in.

If it sells out, don't pay a reseller. The museum says resold tickets aren't valid.

05What to pair it with

The museum is in Mitaka, in west Tokyo, so it fills a morning and pairs well with the neighbourhood around it. In our itinerary it's one of three choices for the "day out of the city":

  • Ghibli Museum + Kichijoji: the museum at 10:00, then Inokashira Park and the shopping streets of Kichijoji.
  • Kamakura + Enoden: ¥1,040 each way by JR from Tokyo since the March 2026 fare change.
  • Kawagoe, "Little Edo".

If Ghibli tickets are gone for your dates, switching to one of the other two is easy. Nothing else on that day needs booking.

06Quick facts

  • Price: ¥1,000 adults, plus ¥110 per ticket from November 2026 entries.
  • Sales: 10th of each month, 10:00 JST, for the next month. Online via Lawson Ticket only.
  • Rules: named tickets, max 6 per account per month, no changes or refunds.
  • Entry: hourly slots 10:00–16:00, enter within 1 hour.
  • Closed: Tuesdays, plus other days that vary by year.
