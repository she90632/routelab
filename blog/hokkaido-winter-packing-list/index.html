Hokkaido winter packing list

Packing for ice, not the ArcticChecked 2026-10-30

Sapporo in winter is cold, but not the kind of cold that needs expedition gear. What catches people out is the ice underfoot and the jump from minus a few degrees outside to a heated train. Pack for those two things.

The short version

  • Daily averages in central Sapporo sit between about −6°C and 0°C from December to February.
  • Boots matter more than anything else: waterproof, warm, with soft rubber soles that grip.
  • Dress in layers you can open up, because shops, trains and the underground walkway are heated.

Two things stand out. Daytime highs hover around zero, so snow that melts in the afternoon freezes again at night, and the pavements turn to polished ice. And the snow stays: average deepest snow cover is 47 cm in December, 76 cm in January and 95 cm in February. December alone brings 113 cm of snowfall over the month, with snow falling on about 28 days.

Mountain resorts and inland towns get colder than this. For a city trip with day trips by train, though, these are the figures to pack for.

02Boots first

If you are short on space, spend it on footwear. You want waterproof boots that keep your feet warm, with soft rubber soles that stay grippy in the cold. The Sapporo City Fire Bureau's advice on avoiding falls in the snow says the same: choose shoes with grippy soles or anti-slip material.

Slip-on cleats help on ice, but the city warns that metal studs become slippery on tiled floors indoors, so take them off when you go inside. A pair that stretches over your boots is easier than boots with built-in studs.

Walking is a skill here. The city recommends small steps, with your weight slightly forward and your whole sole landing at once. Don't rush for a green light. At busy crossings and near stations you will see boxes of grit. Anyone can take a bag and scatter it on an icy patch, so do.

03Layers you can open

Indoors in Sapporo is warm. Department stores, the subway and trains are heated, and a heavy coat you can't unzip becomes a problem within ten minutes. A good setup is a thermal base layer, a fleece or light down jacket, and a waterproof, windproof outer shell with a hood. For long evenings outside, such as the festival nights, add a second warm layer rather than a thicker coat.

Small things go a long way: a hat that covers your ears, a scarf or neck gaiter, and gloves that work with a phone screen, or thin liners under thicker mittens. Pack a spare pair of socks in your day bag. Wet feet are the quickest way to end an afternoon early.

04Small things that earn their space

Disposable hand warmers are sold everywhere in Japan, so buy them there rather than packing them. Lip balm and hand cream for the dry indoor air. Sunglasses if you will be in open snow on a bright day, because the glare is real. A tote or plastic bag for wet boots or gloves when you go into a restaurant or a hotel room.

05The list

Wear on the plane: waterproof boots, thermal base layer, fleece, outer shell. Pack: a second thermal top and leggings, two or three pairs of warm socks plus a spare for the day bag, a hat that covers your ears, a scarf or neck gaiter, touchscreen liners and warm mittens, stretch-on ice cleats, lip balm, hand cream, sunglasses, and a foldable bag for wet things. Buy there: hand warmers, extra socks if you underpacked, and anything else you find you need. Convenience stores and drugstores are everywhere in central Sapporo.

If you are carrying only cabin luggage, wear the bulkiest items on the flight and pack the thin layers. Three thin layers are warmer and more adjustable than one thick one.

06What you can leave at home

A full-length expedition parka and insulated ski trousers are overkill for city sightseeing at these temperatures, unless you plan to ski or stay outdoors for hours. Heavy jeans are a poor choice too: once wet they stay cold. Thermal leggings under ordinary trousers do more for less weight.

07Dates worth planning around

The Sapporo White Illumination lights Odori Park from November 20 to December 25, 2026. The lights are on from 16:30 to 22:00, and until midnight from December 23 to 25.

The 2027 Sapporo Snow Festival runs from February 4 to 11, 2027, at the Odori, Tsudome and Susukino sites. Evenings at the snow sculptures are the coldest part of most trips, which is when the extra layer and the hand warmers pay off.

08Getting around without the ice

Between Sapporo Station and Odori, use Chi-Ka-Ho, the underground walkway of about 520 m that runs under Ekimae-dori. On an icy day it saves you the worst stretch of pavement in the centre.

For trains and buses, an IC card saves fumbling with gloves at ticket machines. JR East says Welcome Suica works in parts of Hokkaido as well as Tokyo. If you are flying in from Tokyo, the card you already have will usually do. Our IC card guide covers the options.
