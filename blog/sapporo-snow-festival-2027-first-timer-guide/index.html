Sapporo Snow Festival 2027

SPK
Feb 4–11, 2027, free entryChecked 2026-10-30

Facts checked 2026-10-30 · 4 min read · Route Lab

AI-assisted content: drafted with AI tools, then fact-checked and edited by Route Lab.

Every February, Sapporo fills its central park with huge snow sculptures, lines a street with ice carvings and turns a sports dome into a snow playground. Entry is free. If it's your first time, the questions are always the same: when is it, where are the sites, how cold will it be, and is the trip to Otaru worth it? Here are the answers, from official sources only.

The short version

  • The 2027 festival runs Feb 4–11 at three free sites: Odori, Susukino and Tsudome.
  • February in Sapporo averages a high of 0.4 °C and a low of −6.2 °C. Boots with grip matter most.
  • The Otaru Snow Light Path runs Feb 6–13, 2027, ¥800 each way on JR from Sapporo.

01Sapporo Snow Festival 2027 dates

The 2027 Sapporo Snow Festival runs February 4–11, 2027, according to the festival's official site. All three sites are free to enter.

A weekend inside that window is the easy choice, but weekends are also the busiest. If you can, go midweek to see the sculptures with fewer people.

02The three sites

Odori site. This is the main event: big snow sculptures along Odori Park in the middle of the city. It's open all day and lit up until 22:00. Try to see it twice, once by day for the detail and once after dark when it's lit. It's walkable from JR Sapporo station, and the food stalls make it an easy lunch stop.

Susukino site. This is the ice-sculpture street in Sapporo's nightlife district. It's lit until 23:00 (22:00 on the last day, Feb 11), so it works well after dinner.

Tsudome site. The family-friendly site, with snow slides and play areas, a little outside the centre. In 2027 it's open 10:00–16:00. It's about 15 minutes on foot from Sakaemachi station on the Toho subway line, or you can take a shuttle bus. In 2026 the shuttle cost ¥200 one way, cash only. The 2027 shuttle hasn't been announced: check before you go.

03How cold is it?

The Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages for Sapporo in February are a daily high of 0.4 °C and a low of −6.2 °C, with about 116 cm of snowfall in the month. Evenings at the sculptures feel colder than that, especially standing still.

What to wear:

  • Thermal base layers, a warm mid layer and an insulated coat that covers your hips
  • A hat, gloves and a scarf. Phone-friendly gloves help for photos
  • Waterproof boots with a deep tread, or clip-on ice grips. Pavements get icy
  • Hand warmers for the night visits

On a bad-weather day, the Sapporo Ekimae-dori underground walkway (Chi-Ka-Ho) links Sapporo and Odori stations indoors. It's free; its opening hours weren't confirmed, so check before you go.

04Getting around Sapporo

The subway is the simplest way to get around. Single fares are ¥210 (zone 1), ¥250 (zone 2) and ¥290 (zone 3). Two day passes, from the Sapporo City Transportation Bureau, save money:

  • Donichika Kippu: ¥520, unlimited subway rides on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays
  • Subway 1-day pass: ¥830 on weekdays

Both are cash only and cover the subway only. The Sapporo tram is separate, at about ¥230 a ride (check before you go).

05Otaru and the Snow Light Path

Otaru is a canal town on the coast, ¥800 each way from Sapporo on JR (from JR Hokkaido's fare table). It makes an easy day trip. In festival season the draw is the Otaru Snow Light Path (Yuki Akari no Michi), Feb 6–13, 2027: snow lanterns along the canal and through the old town, free to walk. The canal area is about 10 minutes on foot from JR Otaru station.

The 2027 lighting hours haven't been published yet. In 2026 it was lit 17:00–21:00, so plan on an evening visit, but check before you go.

Indoor stops in Otaru for daytime include the Bank of Japan Otaru Museum (free, closed Wednesdays), the Otaru Museum and the Otaru Music Box Museum.

06More to do in Sapporo

  • Sapporo TV Tower: the observation deck above Odori, ¥1,200, 9:00–22:00 (last entry 21:50). Expect a wait during the festival.
  • Sapporo Clock Tower: ¥350, 8:45–17:10. It's small, so 20 minutes is enough.
  • Mt Moiwa ropeway and mini cable car: ¥2,100 round trip. Winter hours 11:00–22:00 (last ride up 21:30). It's best for the night view, and it stops in bad weather.
  • Hokkaido Jingu: free. Gates open 7:00–16:00 from Nov 1 to Feb 14. About 15 minutes on foot from Maruyama-koen station.
  • Sapporo Beer Museum: 11:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30). Entry fee and paid tour or tasting prices: check before you go. About 10 minutes on foot from Higashi-kuyakusho-mae station.

07A simple 3-day shape

  1. Day 1, Sapporo: the Odori site by day, the Clock Tower, the TV Tower, then Odori lit up and Susukino's ice sculptures at night.
  2. Day 2, Otaru: JR to the canal, museums in the afternoon, the Snow Light Path after dark.
  3. Day 3, Tsudome and Moiwa: Tsudome in the morning (10:00–16:00), then Mt Moiwa for the night view if the weather allows.

Stay near JR Sapporo station and you can walk to Odori, catch the train to Otaru and reach the subway without changing hotels.
