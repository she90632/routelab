Kyoto autumn foliage 2026

KYO

Kyoto in autumn is the trip many people plan a year ahead. The maples turn, the temples open special halls and evening light-ups, and the city gets very busy. The hard part is timing. Nobody can promise the exact week the leaves will peak. What you can plan around are the dates temples have published for their 2026 autumn openings, and those are firmer than any foliage forecast.

The short version

  • Nobody can promise peak week; colour usually peaks mid-November to early December.
  • Nov 21–30 is when Kiyomizu-dera's night viewing, Eikando's light-up, Tenryu-ji's 7:30 opening and Tofuku-ji's season all overlap.
  • Give southern Higashiyama, northern Higashiyama and Arashiyama a day each.

01When do the leaves change in Kyoto?

This guide sets out those official dates, what the weather is usually like, and how to fit the best temples into a short trip without spending half of it on a bus.

The colour usually peaks from mid-November into early December, but it moves every year with the weather: check before you go. A warm autumn pushes it later, and a cold snap brings it earlier. Different temples also peak at different times, depending on how high up and how shaded they are.

For packing, the Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 averages for Kyoto are a daily high of 17.3 °C and a low of 8.4 °C in November. December drops to 11.6 °C and 3.5 °C. Mornings at an early-opening temple feel colder than the afternoon numbers suggest, so bring layers.

The practical advice is to aim for the last week of November. That's when the most 2026 temple programmes overlap, as the dates below show.

02Official 2026 autumn dates at the big temples

These come from each temple's own website, checked on October 30, 2026. Temples can change hours or limit entry on crowded days, so confirm on the day.

  • Tofuku-ji (Tsutenkyo bridge and Kaisando): autumn season Nov 14 – Dec 6. ¥1,000 per adult, open from 8:30, last entry 16:00. This is Kyoto's famous wooden bridge over a valley of maples, and it gets very crowded. Go at opening.
  • Kiyomizu-dera autumn night viewing: Nov 21–30, 2026. ¥500 per adult, entry 17:30–21:00, gate closes 21:30, no booking needed. The daytime fee isn't on the official site: check before you go.
  • Kodai-ji: ¥800 per adult. The 2026 autumn light-up runs Oct 23 – Dec 13, with lights on from 17:00. One ticket covers day and night, but you can't re-enter once you leave.
  • Eikando: autumn treasure exhibition Nov 11 – Dec 6, ¥1,500, entry 9:00–16:00. The light-up runs Nov 20 – Dec 6, ¥1,000, entry 17:30–20:30. Day and night tickets are separate (you leave and re-enter), and no advance tickets are sold.
  • Tenryu-ji (Arashiyama): garden ¥500 per adult, with a special early opening at 7:30 from Nov 14 to 30, 2026 (garden gate only).
  • Kitano Tenmangu, Momiji-en (maple garden): Oct 31 – Dec 6, 2026, 9:00–16:00 (last entry 15:40). ¥1,500 per adult with tea and a sweet.
  • Toji: autumn opening Oct 31 – Dec 13, 2026, ¥1,200, 8:00–17:00. There's also an evening light-up (¥1,000, 18:00–21:30, last entry 21:00), but its exact dates weren't confirmed: check before you go.

Put those together and Nov 21–30 is the window when Kiyomizu-dera's night viewing, Eikando's light-up, Tenryu-ji's 7:30 opening and Tofuku-ji's autumn season all run at once.

03How to plan the days

Kyoto's foliage spots cluster in three areas. Give each one a day and you'll spend far less time in traffic.

Southern Higashiyama. Start at Tofuku-ji when it opens (JR Nara line to Tofukuji station), then take the bus up to Kodai-ji and the Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka lanes. Finish at Kiyomizu-dera after dark. Kyoto City Bus has a flat ¥230 fare.

Northern Higashiyama. Eikando, Nanzen-ji's Hojo garden (¥600, November hours 8:40–17:00) and the roughly 2 km Philosopher's Path up to Ginkaku-ji (¥1,000; until Nov 30, 8:30–17:00). This day suits the city's Subway & Bus 1-day pass, ¥1,100 per adult.

Arashiyama. Be at Tenryu-ji for 7:30, walk the bamboo grove (free) before the crowds arrive, then go on to Jojakko-ji (¥600, 9:00–17:00). If you'd rather go north, pair Kitano Tenmangu's maple garden with Kinkaku-ji, which opens 9:00–17:00. Its fee isn't shown on the official pages we checked: check before you go.

04Beating the crowds

  • Go early. Opening time at Tofuku-ji, or 7:30 at Tenryu-ji, makes more difference than anything else.
  • Save the evenings for light-ups. Kiyomizu-dera, Eikando and Kodai-ji are often calmer and more atmospheric after dark than at midday.
  • Leave extra time for buses. Autumn traffic in Higashiyama is slow, and a ride that's 10 minutes on the timetable can take far longer. Where a train or subway covers the route, use it.
  • Have a rain plan. Sanjusangendo (¥600) is fully indoors. The Kyoto National Museum's special exhibition "The Tale of Genji" runs Oct 6 – Nov 29, 2026: ¥2,200 at the door, 9:00–17:30, closed Mondays and Tue Nov 24.

05Where to stay

A hotel near Kyoto Station keeps all three days simple: the JR lines to Tofuku-ji and Arashiyama, the subway and the main bus terminal all start there. You won't need to move hotels or carry bags across the city.
