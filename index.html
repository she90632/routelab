02How it works

You tell me how your group likes to travel. I do the research and the routing.

  1. 1
    Send me the basicsDates, city, who's going, where you're staying, the places you can't miss and the ones you'd rather skip.
  2. 2
    I plan the daysTwo or three realistic plans per day, in an order that doesn't zigzag across town. I check opening days and ticket prices on official sites and mark anything I couldn't confirm as an estimate.
  3. 3
    You get one linkSend it to the group chat. It works on any phone or computer and stays online for 12 months.

What the page does

  • Saves each person's picks on their own phone
  • Swaps in the rain plan with one switch
  • Adds up the budget per person as you pick
  • Opens every stop in Google Maps

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't count votes across the group. Everyone's picks stay on their own phone, so tell me the final picks and I'll update the page.
  • I don't book flights, hotels or tickets. You book; I plan.
  • Prices and hours can change after I check them, so confirm before you go.
