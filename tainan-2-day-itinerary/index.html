01Day by day

Each day has two or three plans to choose from and an indoor plan for bad weather. These are the neighbourhoods and the main stops; the times and the order are in the download.

  1. D1

    Day 1: Three routes through the old city

    West Central or Anping

    Salty congee for breakfast, Chihkan Tower, the Martial Temple and Grand Mazu Temple, lunch along Guohua Street and Shennong Street once the lanterns are on. Or the artsy route: the Confucius Temple, Hayashi Department Store, Blueprint Culture Park and Garden Night Market. Or half a day in Anping: the Old Fort, Old Street, the Tree House and the canal at sunset.

    Rain plan: National Museum of Taiwan Literature, Hayashi and the covered Yongle Market

  2. D2

    Day 2: A day trip or a lie-in, train home in the evening

    Further out, or slow

    The Chimei Museum and Tainan Metropolitan Park, easy going with older travellers. Or a hired car to the Sicao Green Tunnel's bamboo rafts, Qigu Salt Mountain and a seafood lunch. Or sleep in, then Shuixian Temple Market, Zhengxing Street and Haian Road.

    Rain plan: A whole day inside the Chimei Museum

02Before you go

Things that change how you plan the trip, from the research behind the itinerary.

  1. Flying into Taoyuan? Skip TaipeiThe Airport MRT stops at THSR Taoyuan Station, where you can board the High Speed Rail straight to Tainan, for a little less than the Taipei fare.
  2. Into town from THSR TainanBus H31 is free with a same-day High Speed Rail ticket; the Shalun Line train is about NT$25 each way.
  3. Book the return train for 18:00 or laterDay 2 runs into the afternoon on every route. A later train means no rushing back from Qigu or the museum.
  4. Match the night market to the dayGarden Night Market, the biggest, opens Thursday to Sunday only. The page tells you which markets are on for the day you pick.
  5. Carry cashPlenty of night-market stalls and small eateries take cash only. A-Tang's congee is mornings only and closes when it sells out.
  6. Afternoon storms are normalEach day has an indoor plan. Note the Chimei Museum closes on Wednesdays and the literature museum on Mondays.
