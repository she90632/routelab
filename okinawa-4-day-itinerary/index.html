01Day by day

Each day has two or three plans to choose from and an indoor plan for bad weather. These are the neighbourhoods and the main stops; the times and the order are in the download.

  1. D1

    Day 1: First night in Naha

    Kokusai-dori and Makishi

    Kokusai-dori, the Heiwa-dori arcade and dinner at Makishi Public Market, where you pick fish downstairs and have it cooked upstairs. Or the pottery lanes of Tsuboya and Okinawan home cooking near Kencho-mae.

    Rain plan: The covered arcades and the market

  2. D2

    Day 2: The castle's rebuilt main hall

    Shuri

    Shuri Castle and its Seiden, open to the public from November 23, 2026, Okinawa soba in Shuri, then the Kinjo-cho stone path downhill. Or the Tamaudun royal tombs and the Okinawa Prefectural Museum, which stays open late on Fridays.

    Rain plan: Inside the Seiden, then the museum

  3. D3

    Day 3: Up north by express bus

    Motobu Peninsula

    About two hours on the Yanbaru Express Bus to Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium, an ocean-view lunch buffet, then the Bise fukugi tree lane. Or Nakijin Castle ruins first and the aquarium in the afternoon.

    Rain plan: A long morning inside the aquarium

  4. D4

    Day 4: A cliff-top shrine, then the airport

    Naha

    Naminoue Shrine above the sea and the Fukushuen garden, or last shopping on Heiwa-dori and Tsuboya, then 13 minutes on Yui Rail to Naha Airport.

    Rain plan: Straight to the airport

02Before you go

Things that change how you plan the trip, from the research behind the itinerary.

  1. Stay near Kencho-maeYui Rail reaches Naha Airport in 13 minutes, and the Yanbaru Express Bus stop at Kencho-kitaguchi is close by. The whole plan is priced from a hotel between Kencho-mae and Makishi.
  2. Book a Seiden time slotGoing inside Shuri Castle's rebuilt main hall needs a timed ticket. December slots went on sale on October 1, first come, first served. The park ticket is ¥1,000 either way.
  3. Leave slack on the bus dayThe Yanbaru Express Bus is paid on board (¥1,900 each way to the aquarium stop) and the operator often posts traffic delays. Don't book anything tight that evening.
  4. Reserve the aquarium lunch by phoneRestaurant Inoh takes phone reservations only and none on the day. The buffet was ¥2,600 for adults.
  5. Know the closing daysThe prefectural museum closes Mondays, Fukushuen Wednesdays, Shuri Soba Thursdays and Sundays (cash only), and Makishi market the 4th Sunday of most months.
  6. Tsuboya's museum is shut until 2027The Tsuboya Pottery Museum is closed for renovation until March 31, 2027. The pottery street itself is open.

04What “no car” means here

Naha days use Yui Rail and walking. The day up north uses the Yanbaru Express Bus, which drops you at the aquarium's stop and at Nakijin Castle.

The plan has no parking or rental-car advice. If you want to drive the coast, this isn't the itinerary for you.
