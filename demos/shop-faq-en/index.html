How to try it

  1. Tap a quick-reply button below the chat, or type your own question, like “Are you open on Mondays?” or “Anything vegan?”
  2. Type an order the way a customer would: “11/8 2 cinnamon rolls and three croissants, pick up 3pm”. It gets split into items, quantities, and pickup date and time, then written into the sheet on the right (on a phone, it's under the “Order sheet” tab).
  3. When the customer replies “confirm”, the status goes from “Pending” to “Confirmed”. Click a status in the sheet to change it to “Picked up” or “Cancelled”.
  4. Use the menu at the top right of the chat to switch customers and see how several orders get sorted by date, status, and item.

How the real version connects

In production, the chat side runs on the LINE Messaging API: the shop's Official Account points a webhook at a small server, which applies the same keyword rules to each customer message and hands anything it can't answer to a real person. The order side writes into the shop's own spreadsheet through the Google Sheets API. This demo isn't connected to any account and has no API keys — every message and order lives only in this browser's localStorage, and clearing it wipes everything.
